# Privacy Policy

EZPowers is a local Codex workflow plugin. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data on its own.

The plugin contains local instructions, skills, and workflow guidance. Any data sent to Codex or OpenAI services is governed by the user's Codex/OpenAI configuration and the services they choose to use.

If future EZPowers features add telemetry, network calls, or external integrations, they must be documented here before release.
